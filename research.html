  1. Saddlepoint approximations for plug-in resampling

    Biometrika • Advance article (asag063) • 2026

    Access: Journal version | arXiv | R Package

    TL;DR

    When a resampling test only re-draws a sum of conditionally independent terms (with nuisance fits held fixed), you don’t need to resample at all: a saddlepoint approximation gives the p-value analytically, with a finite-sample relative-error guarantee. For the distilled conditional randomization test this matches resampling-based p-values while running up to 250× faster.
